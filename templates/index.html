      About HAM-C

      HAM-C is a wrapper around ChatGPT. You chat here instead of on the ChatGPT website. It talks to ChatGPT through an OpenAI API key and shows the replies in this app.

      Memory is handled by HAM-C, not by ChatGPT Memory. When you share a lasting detail, HAM-C saves it in the Fact store, can reuse it in later chats, and shows which fact was used under the answer.

      If you delete a chat, the facts that came from that chat are removed too — so HAM-C will not keep remembering them.

      That makes it easier to see and manage what the assistant knows about you, without needing a separate ChatGPT login.
